Lead Management Workflow – Professional Summary
This workflow is an automated lead management system designed to handle client inquiries from start to finish in a professional and efficient manner.
How it works:
•	When a new client submits a form (via Google Sheets), the workflow is triggered.
•	The AI Agent (powered by Google Gemini + LangChain) processes the submission to:
o	Create a polished summary of the client’s requirement.
o	Identify the services the client is requesting.
o	Assign a lead score (High/Medium/Low) based on intent and clarity.
o	Detect urgency (Urgent, Normal, or Exploratory).
o	Draft a polite acknowledgment message for the client.
o	Generate a structured summary for the owner.
•	All of this processed data is saved in the Company Response Sheet for easy tracking and follow-up.
Automated communication:
•	A thank-you email is immediately sent to the client, confirming their inquiry.
•	A detailed notification email is sent to the owner, including the client’s information, services requested, urgency, lead score, and summary.
Business impact:
•	Saves time by removing manual effort in handling new leads.
•	Ensures clients always receive timely and consistent communication.
•	Gives the owner clear, structured insights for faster decision-making.
•	Improves lead response speed, professionalism, and overall conversion potential.
